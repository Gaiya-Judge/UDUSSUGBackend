About the System

The UDUS SUG Elections platform lets registered students vote securely from any device, and records every vote on a blockchain so that results can be checked by anyone.

Why we built it

Manual elections mean long queues, slow verification, slow counting and little transparency. This system removes the queues, counts instantly, and makes the results independently verifiable.

How it works

1

Verify

Enter your admission number. A one-time code is sent to the email linked to it.

2

Vote

Choose a candidate for each position, or abstain, then review your choices.

3

Record

Your vote is submitted to the election smart contract and you receive a Receipt ID.

4

Results

When voting ends, results appear automatically on the Results page, read directly from the smart contract.

Our principles

Transparent

Results are read directly from the smart contract, with no manual publishing step.

Fair

Every eligible student gets one ballot, and the candidate list is locked on the blockchain once voting begins.

Credible

Votes are recorded as blockchain transactions that cannot be edited or deleted.

Developed as a final-year project at Usmanu Danfodiyo University, Sokoto.
